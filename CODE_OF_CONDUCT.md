# Community code of conduct

LoopGuard’s community should be safe, precise, and welcoming. Treat contributors with respect
regardless of background, identity, experience, employer, or technical preference.

Expected behavior includes constructive technical disagreement, patient explanations, clear
evidence, respect for privacy, and taking responsibility for mistakes. Unacceptable behavior
includes harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, doxxing, sharing
private prompts or source, deliberately unsafe security testing, or sustained disruption.

Maintainers may edit, hide, reject, or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when needed to protect people or the project. Enforcement decisions should
consider context, impact, pattern, and repair—not popularity.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Do not include sensitive
security details; use the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for vulnerabilities. Maintainers
will limit disclosure to people needed to evaluate and respond, while recognizing that absolute
confidentiality cannot be guaranteed.
